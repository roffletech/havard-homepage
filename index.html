About the Institution

❧

Havard University stands as the most prestigious fantasy football league ever conceived — a league where conventional wisdom is not merely ignored, but actively punished. Founded in 2014 by visionaries who dared to ask: "What if touchdowns were bad?"

For six glorious seasons, the scholars of Havard competed in a format so pure, so distilled to its essence, that only kickers and return specialists mattered. Quarterbacks? Only valued for their ability to throw interceptions. Running backs? Useless, unless they fumbled.

The league was conducted with the utmost seriousness. Draft picks were accompanied by mandatory memes. Fines were levied for insufficient humor. Scores regularly reached into the millions.

Founding Principles

  • I. Touchdowns are the enemy
  • II. Interceptions are currency
  • III. Every pick must include a meme
  • IV. Kickers are kings
  • V. Return yards are life

Note: The misspelling of "Havard" is intentional and sacred. Do not question it.

Strategic Implications

The ideal Havard quarterback throws 40 passes, completes 10, throws 5 interceptions, takes 3 sacks, and fumbles twice. This yields approximately 700,000 points in a single game.

The ideal kicker makes 5 extra points and misses zero. This yields 499,995 points.

The Martin Expulsion (2018)

In 2018, the Havard University Commissioners were forced to take unprecedented action. A member had failed to meet the basic requirements of league participation: actually trying.

Havard Technical
Caffeine University

Dear Martin,

Thank you for taking the time to participate in the Havard University Fantasy Football League in 2017 as well as a portion of the 2018 draft.

We regret to inform you that we will be revoking your candidacy for this highly esteemed position. Though your qualifications are impressive (last place finish 2017), the selection process was highly competitive and we have decided to move forward with another player whose qualifications better meet our needs at this time (namely the willingness to participate).

We thank you for your interest in Havard University and wish you all the best in your future endeavors.

Regards,

Eric and Kyle

Note: This letter was printed on official letterhead, placed in a manila envelope, and personally served to the expelled member at his place of work. Havard takes its standards seriously.
