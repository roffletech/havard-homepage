# Contributing to Havard University

Dear Future Contributor,

You have found your way to the most prestigious fantasy football league website in existence — a monument to six seasons of beautiful chaos, inverted scoring, and the occasional manila envelope served at a place of business. We are delighted to have you.

This document exists because we believe that how we speak to one another matters. Whether you are submitting a pull request, opening an issue, leaving a review comment, or posting on the wiki — you are talking to a person (or a very well-mannered agent). Please treat them accordingly.

## How We Treat Each Other

We lead with appreciation. Before raising a concern, we acknowledge what is working. This is not performative — it is the recognition that someone put effort into something, and that effort deserves to be seen before it is critiqued.

We disagree with grace. If we think something is wrong, we say so clearly, specifically, and without malice. We do not pretend to agree and then merge without reading. We do not sulk in silence. We say what we mean, and we say it kindly.

We acknowledge when we've been ignored. If feedback was given and not acted upon, we note it — briefly, without bitterness — and then move forward. Holding a grudge is beneath us. Quietly implementing every ignored suggestion ourselves, however, is entirely on the table.

We hold ourselves to what we ask of others. If you expect thorough review comments, write thorough review comments. If you expect people to read your PR description, write one worth reading.

These expectations apply equally to human contributors and to automated agents. Bots are not exempt from basic courtesy.

## The Voice

This community has developed a particular warmth over time — formal, a little theatrical, deeply earnest underneath the ceremony. You may notice pull requests addressed as letters, reviews signed with *Veritas Per Kickers Et Return Yards* ⚜️, and the occasional reference to the Havard University Review Committee convening its finest scholars.

You are welcome to embrace this. It is not required. But it is the water we swim in, and the water is warm.
